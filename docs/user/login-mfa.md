# Amdox ERP User Manuals Guide

This guide covers system access, dashboard analytics, and core operational modules navigation.

---

## 1. Authentication & MFA Access
1. Navigate to the login portal.
2. Provide your email credentials.
3. Configure your TOTP Authenticator app using the scanned QR code.
4. Input the active 6-digit MFA verification code to confirm access.

---

## 2. Module Navigation Directories
- **Dashboard**: View system performance, sales metrics, and task approvals widgets.
- **CRM & Sales**: Pipeline tracking, leads records, opportunities board, and sales orders grids.
- **Procurement & Inventory**: Purchase order cycles, supplier reviews, product lists, and warehouse bin transfers.
- **Manufacturing & Quality**: Work centers, routing processes, BOM checklists, inspection plan results, and NCR/CAPA logs.
- **HRM, Payroll & ESS**: Organization hierarchy tree, attendance checks, expense requests filing, payslip PDF downloads, and goal settings.
- **Finance & Accounting**: Chart of Accounts configuration, journal entries reconciliation, and consolidated company mergers.
- **BI & AI Tools**: BI dashboard analytics charts, and AI predictive anomaly alerts.
